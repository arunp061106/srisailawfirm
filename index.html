What We Do

Our Practice Areas

We offer comprehensive legal services across key domains, ensuring every client receives expert guidance tailored to their specific needs.

🏦

SARFAESI Act & DRT

Specialised representation in SARFAESI proceedings and Debt Recovery Tribunal cases for both borrowers and financial institutions.

  • SARFAESI Act challenges & notices
  • DRT & DRAT appeals
  • Securitisation disputes
  • Loan recovery proceedings
  • Asset possession challenges
⚖

Civil Litigation

Robust representation in all civil disputes, from contract matters to injunctions, executed with strategic precision.

  • Contract disputes & breach of agreement
  • Injunctions & stay orders
  • Money recovery suits
  • Specific performance suits
  • Execution petitions
👨‍👩‍👧

Family Law

Sensitive and skilled handling of family legal matters — protecting your rights while preserving dignity through difficult times.

  • Divorce & matrimonial disputes
  • Child custody & guardianship
  • Maintenance & alimony
  • Domestic violence cases
  • Succession & inheritance
🏠

Property Law

End-to-end legal assistance in property transactions, disputes, and documentation to safeguard your investments.

  • Title verification & due diligence
  • Property dispute resolution
  • Sale deed & agreement drafting
  • Encroachment & eviction suits
  • Partition suits
🏢

Labour & Banking Law

Expert counsel on employment matters and banking law disputes — balancing the rights of employees and financial entities.

  • Wrongful termination cases
  • Provident Fund & ESI disputes
  • Cheque dishonour (NI Act Sec.138)
  • Banking fraud & recovery
  • Loan & guarantee disputes
💬

Legal Consultation

Professional legal advice and documentation services for individuals and businesses — clear, confidential, and actionable.

  • One-on-one legal advisory
  • Legal notice drafting
  • Contract review & vetting
  • Affidavit & document preparation
  • Case evaluation & strategy
